Tips for Precise Measurement

  • Use a flexible tape measure: Wrap it snugly (but not tightly) around your body without squeezing.
  • Measure bare skin or light clothing: Bulky shirts or pants will skew size results.
  • Ask a friend to help: Height and chest are much easier to measure when standing naturally while someone else takes the coordinates.
  • Metric vs Imperial: Our models convert all data to a single unified scale internally, so feel free to choose whichever scale you prefer.
