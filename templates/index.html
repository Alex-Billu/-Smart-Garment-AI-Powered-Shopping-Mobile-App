About Smart Garment

Welcome to Smart Garment, where state-of-the-art technology meets high-end fashion design. Founded in 2026, we noticed a major problem in online shopping: sizing inconsistency between brands. Over 30% of online garment orders are returned due to poor fitting, which leads to high shipping emissions and customer dissatisfaction.

Our solution is an AI-powered shopping engine. By building a custom Random Forest Classifier trained on hundreds of body measurements and fit preferences, we provide customized sizing predictions that guarantee a perfect look and feel.
